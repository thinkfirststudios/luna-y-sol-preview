curl-first, full service

Services

From dry-cut curly transformations to lived-in balayage, everything we do is tailored to your texture and your life. No menu pricing, just work that fits your hair.

Curly & wavy cuts

our specialty

The cornerstone of everything we do. Using the dry-cutting method, we sculpt each cut to the character of your curls, maximizing spring, minimizing bulk, and giving you a shape that wears beautifully on day one and day five.

No two curl patterns are alike, and no two cuts should be either.

Cuts & styling

for every texture

Precision cuts for all hair types, from blunt bobs to long layers. We also offer blowouts and special-occasion styling for when you want an elevated, editorial look, whether it's a dinner reservation or a wedding morning.

Color

lived-in & luminous

Sun-kissed color that moves the way you do: balayage, highlights, single-process color, glosses and toners. Always designed to flatter your skin tone and grow out gracefully.

Great color should look effortless, not like it's trying too hard.

Texture services

transform or smooth

Perms for clients who want waves and curls, Brazilian blowouts, and professional smoothing treatments, all tailored to your hair's needs and your day-to-day routine.

Extensions

length & volume

Extension application and ongoing maintenance for added length, volume, or both. We match your natural texture and color for a seamless result that moves and feels like your own hair.

Pricing depends on length, density and the complexity of your service. Book a consultation and we'll give you a personal quote.
